# Zomato Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato's customer, order, and restaurant data using **SQL, Google BigQuery, and Google Sheets** to understand overall business performance and customer behaviour.

The analysis covers **revenue, customer analysis, restaurant performance, coupon usage, cancellations, and customer churn**.

## Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering key business questions related to:

- Revenue trends and performance
- Customer value and repeat behaviour
- Restaurant and cuisine performance
- Coupon usage and retention
- Order cancellations and associated revenue
- Customer churn and previously generated revenue

## Tools Used

- **Google Sheets** – Initial data cleaning and data inspection
- **Google BigQuery** – Data storage and analysis
- **SQL** – Data extraction, transformation, aggregation, and business analysis

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Revenue Analysis

- Total delivered revenue
- Monthly revenue trends
- Revenue by city
- Revenue by payment mode
- Average Order Value

### 2. Customer Analysis

- Top 20 customers by revenue
- Revenue contribution of top customers
- Customer acquisition channels
- Repeat customers

### 3. Restaurant Performance

- Highest-revenue restaurants
- Restaurants with the highest number of orders
- Popular cuisines
- Rating vs. revenue
- Lowest-performing restaurants

### 4. Coupon Analysis

- Coupon usage percentage
- Coupon vs. non-coupon order behaviour
- Coupon usage by city
- Coupon usage and customer retention

### 5. Cancellation & Refund Analysis

- Cancellation rate
- Refund rate
- Revenue associated with cancelled orders
- Restaurants with higher cancellation rates

### 6. Customer Churn Analysis

- Number of churned customers
- Churn rate
- Churn by city
- Revenue previously generated by churned customers
- High-value churned customers

## Key Findings

- Total delivered revenue was approximately **₹26.86 million**.
- **Noida** generated the highest city-level revenue at approximately **₹3.60 million**.
- **UPI** generated the highest revenue among payment modes.
- **Instagram Ads** acquired the highest number of customers.
- **Burger King** recorded the highest brand-level revenue and order count.
- **North Indian** was the most ordered cuisine.
- Approximately **40.32%** of delivered orders involved a discount.
- Coupon users showed higher observed retention than non-coupon users.

## Key Assumptions

- `discount_amount > 0` is treated as coupon usage.
- A repeat customer has more than one delivered order.
- A customer is considered churned when their last order was more than 90 days before the latest order date in the dataset.
- Cancelled order value is treated as potential revenue lost due to cancellations.

## Conclusion

This project provides a comprehensive view of Zomato's business performance across **revenue, customers, restaurants, coupons, cancellations, and churn**, helping identify patterns and areas for potential business improvement.
